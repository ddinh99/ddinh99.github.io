Kingrow Privacy Policy

App: Kingrow - Checkers & Draughts (com.daigame.kingrow) · Developer: Dai Dinh · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Kingrow collects, why, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data.

Playing offline

Games against the computer, two-player games on one device, lessons, puzzles, the campaign and settings work without an account. Your progress, coins and settings are stored on your device.

Online play

When you use online features (Multiplayer, Quick Match, invites, the leaderboard), the app signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and stores:

  • An anonymous user ID created automatically.
  • Your player name (a generated name such as "Swift Walnut 42", or one you type, checked against a word filter), the preset portrait you pick, and game statistics (rating, wins, losses).
  • Online game rooms: moves, clocks, results, and the quick-chat phrases you send (only fixed phrases; there is no free-text chat).
  • Players you block and reports you submit about other players.

This data is used only to run the online features. Your player name, portrait and statistics are visible to other players.

Saving your progress (optional)

If you choose Save your progress, your anonymous account is linked to Sign in with Google, Sign in with Apple, or an email address and password, so you can restore it on another device. We receive your account's email address and user ID from that provider; passwords are handled by Firebase and never visible to us. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Local network play

Playing a friend on the same Wi-Fi uses your local network to find and connect to their device. This traffic stays on your local network and is not stored by us.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and information about ad interactions to show, measure and limit ads, and, where you consent, to personalise them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, OS version, app version and technical details of the crash) so we can fix bugs. These reports do not include your name or email.

Data sharing and security

Data is processed by Google (Firebase and AdMob) on our behalf (Firebase privacy information). It is sent over encrypted connections. We do not share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its profile data from our servers. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

If you can't access the app, email ddinh99@gmail.com with your player name (or the email address you saved your progress with), and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Children

Kingrow is intended for users 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the online features, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: ddinh99@gmail.com.
