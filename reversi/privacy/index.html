Inversio Privacy Policy

App: Inversio - Reversi Board Game (com.daigame.inversio) · Developer: Dai Dinh · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information Inversio collects, why, and how you can delete it. Inversio has no ads and does not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Playing offline

Games against the computer, the tutorial, puzzles, the campaign and settings work without an account. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Online play and accounts

When you use online features (playing friends, invites, friends list, messages, leaderboard), the app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • An anonymous user ID created automatically, or your email address and password if you create an account (passwords are handled by Firebase and never visible to us).
  • Your display name, avatar choice and game statistics (rating, wins, losses).
  • Online game rooms and moves, your friends and friend requests, and messages you send to other players.
  • Your online status (whether you are currently in the app), so friends can see when you can play.
  • Reports you submit about other players.

This data is used only to run the online features. Your display name, avatar and statistics are visible to other players; your email address is not shown to them.

Crash reports

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, OS version, app version and technical details of the crash) so we can fix bugs. These reports do not include your name or email.

Data sharing and security

Data is processed by Google Firebase on our behalf (Firebase privacy information). It is sent over encrypted connections. We do not share it with anyone else, except if required by law.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Account and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its profile data from our servers. If anything remains that you want removed (for example messages), email us and we will delete it. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

If you can't access the app, email ddinh99@gmail.com from the address on your account (or include your display name), and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Children

Inversio is intended for users 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: ddinh99@gmail.com.
